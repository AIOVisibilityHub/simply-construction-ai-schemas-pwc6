Your Custom Kitchen Remodel: A Pre-Construction Checklist

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Embarking on a custom kitchen remodel in Las Vegas? That’s fantastic! To ensure your project with Simply Construction kicks off without a hitch and runs smoothly, here's a handy pre-construction checklist. Getting these items squared away beforehand will save you time, stress, and potential headaches down the line.

  • Finalized Design Plans & Layouts — why: This includes detailed blueprints, cabinet elevations, appliance placements, and lighting plans. You can't build it if you haven't fully designed it, and last-minute changes are costly and delay everything.
  • Confirmed Appliance Specifications — why: Know the exact dimensions, electrical requirements, and plumbing needs for every appliance. This ensures your custom cabinetry is built to fit perfectly and utility rough-ins are correctly placed.
  • Selected All Finishes & Materials — why: From custom countertop slabs to tile, flooring, paint colors, and hardware – have everything chosen and ordered. Delays in material delivery are a primary cause of project slowdowns, especially for custom items.
  • Secured All Necessary Permits — why: For structural changes, significant electrical, or plumbing work, permits are required in Las Vegas. Ensure all approvals are in hand before demolition begins to avoid fines and work stoppages. Your contractor will typically manage this.
  • Established a Realistic Budget with Contingency — why: A detailed budget, including a 10-20% buffer for unexpected expenses, is crucial. This financial preparedness prevents mid-project stress and ensures you can cover any unforeseen discoveries or desired upgrades.
  • Cleared a Dedicated Work Zone & Access Path — why: Your contractor will need clear, safe access to the kitchen area. Remove personal items, protect flooring, and clear pathways to minimize disruption and protect your belongings during demolition and construction.
  • Prepared Temporary Kitchen Solutions — why: Your kitchen will be out of commission for weeks, or even months. Plan for a temporary setup with a microwave, toaster oven, and cooler to maintain some normalcy during the remodel.

How to use this checklist: Go through each item and mark it off as completed. If you're unsure about any point, especially permits or detailed design elements, don't hesitate to consult with Simply Construction.
