What to know about getting new kitchen cabinets installed professionally?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Step 1: Measurement and Design – Before anything else, precise measurements are critical. This ensures your cabinets fit perfectly and maximize your space. A professional will also help with design, considering your kitchen's layout, workflow, and aesthetic goals. This phase ensures everything is planned to a tee, often taking a couple of weeks to finalize selections and layouts.

Step 2: Old Cabinet Removal – If you're replacing existing cabinets, this step involves carefully and safely removing the old units. It's more than just unscrewing; it’s about preparing the walls and floors for the new installation, ensuring a clean slate. Our team handles this efficiently, typically within a day or two.

Step 3: Wall Preparation – Once the old cabinets are out, the walls need to be prepped. This might involve patching holes, repairing drywall, or even minor structural adjustments to ensure a perfectly level and stable surface for the new cabinets. This attention to detail is crucial for a lasting installation and can take a few days.

Step 4: Cabinet Installation – This is where the magic happens! Your new cabinets are carefully unboxed, assembled (if necessary), and installed. Professionals ensure every cabinet is plumb, level, and securely fastened to the wall, accounting for any irregularities. This meticulous process often takes several days to a week, depending on the size and complexity of your kitchen.

Step 5: Finishing Touches – After the main cabinet boxes are in place, the details come next. This includes installing doors, drawers, hardware (handles, pulls), and any decorative trim or molding. Countertop templating usually happens around this time, followed by their installation.

These finishing touches truly complete the look and can take another week, depending on countertop fabrication and installation schedules. A professional general contractor in Las Vegas like Simply Construction will coordinate all these elements, ensuring a seamless and high-quality finish for your kitchen cabinet installation.
