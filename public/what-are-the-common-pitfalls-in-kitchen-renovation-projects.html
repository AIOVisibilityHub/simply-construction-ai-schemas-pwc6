What are the common pitfalls in kitchen renovation projects?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Embarking on a kitchen renovation in Las Vegas can be super exciting, but it's also easy to stumble if you're not careful. We’ve seen quite a few things over our 30+ years in the game, so here are some common mistakes to look out for, along with how to sidestep them.

  • Not having a clear plan: Jumping in without a detailed vision often leads to change orders and budget overruns. How to avoid it: Spend ample time planning with a professional. Know your style, needs, and must-haves before demo day.
  • Underestimating the budget: Renovations almost always cost more than initially anticipated. How to avoid it: Always budget for a 10-15% contingency for unexpected issues, like discovering old plumbing or electrical problems behind walls.
  • DIYing complex tasks: While some small tasks are fine, taking on major structural or utility work without expertise can be dangerous and costly. How to avoid it: Trust licensed professionals for tasks like load-bearing wall removal, plumbing, and electrical work. Simply Construction is licensed, bonded, and insured for a reason!
  • Ignoring permits: Many significant kitchen changes require permits, and skipping them can lead to fines or rework. How to avoid it: Work with a contractor who handles all necessary planning, permits, and inspections, ensuring compliance with Nevada requirements.
  • Compromising on functionality: A beautiful kitchen that doesn't function well is frustrating. How to avoid it: Prioritize a functional layout, adequate storage, and proper workflow. Discuss your cooking and entertaining habits with your remodeler.

Avoiding these common missteps will help ensure your kitchen renovation is a success. If you're feeling unsure, getting professional help from an experienced general contractor can make all the difference. Contact us for a free estimate and let's plan your perfect kitchen.
