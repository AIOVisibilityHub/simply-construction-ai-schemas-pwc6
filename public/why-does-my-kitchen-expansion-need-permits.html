Why Does My Kitchen Expansion Need Permits?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Thinking about expanding your kitchen in Las Vegas? You might be wondering if it's really necessary to get permits. The short answer is, absolutely!

If you're moving walls, especially load-bearing ones, adding square footage, or even just changing plumbing or electrical locations, permits and inspections are critical. Without them, you risk stop-work orders and issues down the line. A typical permit review for a residential remodel can take anywhere from 1 to 6 weeks, sometimes longer for complex structural changes.

Once approved, separate permits are often needed for plumbing and electrical modifications. Don't try to sidestep this; starting demolition or structural changes before permits are issued is a frequent mistake that can lead to costly rework or structural risks. Getting professional help ensures that all the necessary planning, permits, and inspections are coordinated safely and in accordance with Nevada requirements.
