Why Your Kitchen Remodel Budget Needs a Cushion

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

It’s super exciting to plan a kitchen remodel in your Las Vegas home, and naturally, you’ve got a budget in mind. But here’s a pro tip: always, always factor in a contingency. Without that financial cushion, unexpected twists can quickly turn your dream kitchen into a budget nightmare.

Why is a contingency so crucial? First, during demolition, you might uncover hidden issues like outdated wiring, plumbing problems, or even structural surprises that weren't visible before. These things absolutely need to be addressed, and they cost money and time.

Second, you might fall in love with a slightly pricier finish or appliance that wasn't in your initial plan – and you’ll want the flexibility to upgrade without derailing your entire project. Third, lead times for custom items like cabinets can sometimes be unpredictable, or supply chain issues might force a quicker, more expensive substitute. A 10-20% contingency is generally recommended for kitchen remodels.

It allows you to tackle unforeseen challenges, make minor adjustments, or even splurge a little without stress. Ignoring this can lead to cutting corners mid-project or worse, leaving your kitchen unfinished.

Planning your budget effectively, including that vital contingency, helps ensure your kitchen remodel stays on track.
