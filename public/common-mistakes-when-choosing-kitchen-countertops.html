Common mistakes when choosing kitchen countertops?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Picking out new kitchen countertops for your Las Vegas home is a big decision, and it’s easy to get overwhelmed with all the choices out there. To help you avoid some common pitfalls, here are a few mistakes we often see, along with how to steer clear of them:

  • Choosing solely based on aesthetics without considering durability: That beautiful countertop might look amazing, but if it's going into a high-traffic kitchen with kids and daily cooking, you need a material that can stand up to the wear and tear. Always balance beauty with practicality for your lifestyle. Consider materials like quartz or granite for their robustness.
  • Forgetting about maintenance requirements: Some materials require more sealing, special cleaners, or are more prone to staining. Don’t fall in love with a countertop only to realize its upkeep is too much for you. Ask about maintenance upfront and choose a material that fits your willingness to clean and seal.
  • Ignoring the overall kitchen design and color palette: Your countertops are a major visual element, and they need to complement your cabinets, flooring, and wall colors. Bringing samples home and viewing them in your kitchen's lighting is crucial to avoid a mismatched look. A cohesive design elevates the entire space.
  • Not budgeting for installation and other associated costs: The price of the slab isn't the only expense. You also need to account for fabrication, delivery, installation, and potentially a new sink or faucet. Get a comprehensive quote to avoid surprises down the line. A reputable general contractor like Simply Construction will provide a clear breakdown.
  • Trying to DIY a complex installation: While some small projects might be DIY-friendly, installing large, heavy, or custom-cut countertops is a job best left to the professionals. Precision is key to a seamless look and proper function. Incorrect installation can lead to costly damage or an uneven finish. Save yourself the headache and trust the experts for a flawless kitchen countertop installation.
