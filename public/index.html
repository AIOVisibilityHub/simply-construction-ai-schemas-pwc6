About Simply Construction

Simply Construction is a full-service, licensed general contractor in Las Vegas, Nevada, providing remodeling, renovation, construction, repair, and improvement services for residential, multi-family, investment, and small commercial properties. We are licensed, bonded, and insured, with more than 30 years of hands-on experience helping property owners plan, permit, build, renovate, and maintain projects of all sizes.
