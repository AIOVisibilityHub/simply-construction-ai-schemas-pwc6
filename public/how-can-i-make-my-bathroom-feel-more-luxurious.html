How can I make my bathroom feel more luxurious?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Transforming your bathroom into a luxurious retreat doesn't always require a complete overhaul, though a full remodel definitely opens up possibilities! It's often about thoughtful details and smart upgrades that elevate the space. One great place to start is with lighting.

Ditch harsh overhead lights for dimmable fixtures, elegant sconces, or even integrated vanity lighting. This creates a spa-like ambiance. Next, consider upgrading your shower experience.

A walk-in shower with multiple showerheads, body jets, or even a steam function can feel incredibly indulgent. Simply Construction offers expert tub-to-shower conversions and custom tile work that can make your shower the focal point. Material choices also play a huge role.

Think about high-quality tiles for floors and walls, a beautiful natural stone countertop for your vanity, or even custom cabinetry. These elements add sophistication and durability. Don't underestimate the power of a new, stylish vanity.

We can install vanities that provide ample storage and a touch of elegance. Lastly, consider details like heated floors for those chilly Las Vegas mornings, high-end fixtures, or a freestanding soaking tub if space allows. Even new, fluffy towels and a curated selection of toiletries can enhance the feeling of luxury.

We serve areas like MacDonald Highlands, Southern Highlands, and Inspirada, and our team can help you design a bathroom that feels like your personal sanctuary.
