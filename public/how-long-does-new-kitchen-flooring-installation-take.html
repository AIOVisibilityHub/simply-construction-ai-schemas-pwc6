How long does new kitchen flooring installation take?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Step 1: Preparation (1-2 days). This involves clearing out your kitchen – removing appliances, furniture, and anything else in the way. Then, the existing flooring needs to be carefully removed. This can be quick for some materials like vinyl, but older, heavily glued tiles or subfloor issues might take longer. We also need to ensure the subfloor is clean, level, and ready for the new material.

Step 2: Subfloor Repair/Leveling (0.5-2 days). If the subfloor isn't perfectly flat or has damage, it'll need repair or leveling compounds applied. This is a critical step for a smooth, long-lasting new floor, and drying times for leveling compounds need to be factored in.

Step 3: New Flooring Installation (1-5 days). This is where the magic happens! The actual laying of the new flooring. For smaller kitchens with straightforward layouts and materials like LVP or laminate, it might be on the shorter side. Larger kitchens, intricate tile patterns, or materials requiring precise cuts and grouting (like ceramic or porcelain tile) will naturally take longer. For tile, you also have grouting and sealing, which adds extra time.

So, for a typical kitchen, you're generally looking at anywhere from 3 to 10 days from start to finish, depending on the complexity, materials, and any unforeseen subfloor issues.
