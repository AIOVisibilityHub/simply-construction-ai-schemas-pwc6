When Should I Call a Pro for My Bathroom Renovation?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

A bathroom renovation can be a big undertaking, and knowing when to call in a professional can save you a lot of stress and money. Here are some key signs it's time to bring in the experts:

  • You're changing plumbing locations or adding new fixtures: Moving supply lines, drains, or adding a new shower or toilet almost always triggers plumbing permits and inspections. Pros ensure everything meets code for slope, venting, and spacing.
  • New electrical circuits, lighting, or exhaust fans are involved: Any electrical work, especially adding new circuits or relocating fixtures, needs to comply with electrical codes. An electrician will ensure safety and proper functionality, and typically requires a permit.
  • You're building a wet-area, like a tiled shower or curbless entry: Waterproofing in these areas is crucial, and if done incorrectly, can lead to costly leaks and hidden damage. A professional knows the right systems and sequencing to guarantee a watertight seal.
  • Structural elements are being modified: If you're moving walls, cutting joists, or altering floor framing, you're dealing with structural components. This requires expertise to ensure your home's integrity and often involves structural engineers and specific inspections.
  • You only have one bathroom in the house: When downtime is critical, a professional contractor can coordinate trades efficiently, ensuring the project stays on schedule and minimizing the time your essential bathroom is out of commission.
