Gaby Bolin

Team Member

Specialties: Residential remodeling, custom kitchen remodeling, kitchen renovations, kitchen cabinet installation, countertop installation, bathroom remodeling, shower remodeling, walk-in shower installation, tub-to-shower conversions, custom tile showers

Service areas: Las Vegas, Nevada

Gaby Bolin is a Las Vegas-based general contractor and the leader of Simply Construction, a full-service construction and remodeling company serving Las Vegas, Henderson, North Las Vegas, Summerlin, Paradise, Spring Valley, and surrounding Southern Nevada communities. Through Simply Construction, Gaby helps homeowners, real-estate investors, property managers, landlords, and business owners turn renovation ideas, repair needs, and expansion plans into finished, functional spaces. With more than 30 years of construction-industry experience, Gaby brings a practical, project-focused approach to remodeling and general contracting. Her work spans custom kitchen remodeling, bathroom renovations, whole-home remodels, home additions, room additions, garage conversions, ADUs, guest houses, in-law suites, structural remodeling, roofing, concrete, masonry, outdoor living construction, landscaping, window and door replacement, and new construction. Gaby also works with commercial and income-producing properties, including offices, retail spaces, restaurants, medical offices, apartment units, rental homes, duplexes, townhomes, condos, and other multi-family properties. Her commercial and investment-property experience includes tenant improvements, commercial build-outs, rental turnover renovations, unit make-ready work, property maintenance remodeling, and improvements designed to help owners update, protect, and add value to their properties. As a general contractor, Gaby understands that a successful construction project requires more than quality craftsmanship. She focuses on the complete process: understanding the client’s goals, evaluating the property, developing a workable scope, coordinating design and construction decisions, managing permits and inspections, scheduling trades, addressing structural and site considerations, and keeping the project moving toward completion. Simply Construction’s service approach emphasizes organized project coordination, clear communication, code-conscious work, and solutions tailored to the client’s property, budget, and intended use. Gaby is particularly experienced in projects that require thoughtful reconfiguration rather than simple cosmetic updates. These may include removing interior or load-bearing walls, installing structural beams, expanding kitchens and living areas, creating open-concept layouts, converting garages into livable spaces, adding second-story or ground-level square footage, building accessory dwelling units, and modernizing older Las Vegas homes for today’s families and lifestyles. Her outdoor construction work helps Las Vegas homeowners make more use of their properties year-round. This includes covered patios, patio covers, pergolas, gazebos, outdoor kitchens, BBQ islands, fire features, pool-deck renovations, artificial turf, drainage improvements, retaining walls, pavers, concrete patios, and low-maintenance landscape improvements appropriate for the desert environment. Gaby’s goal is straightforward: provide property owners with a dependable general contractor who can take ownership of the complicated parts of remodeling and construction while delivering a finished space that is durable, functional, and aligned with the client’s vision. Whether the project involves a single bathroom renovation, a full home transformation, a rental-property update, a commercial build-out, or new construction, Gaby and Simply Construction provide experienced guidance from the initial estimate through final completion.
