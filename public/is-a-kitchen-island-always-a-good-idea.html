Is a kitchen island always a good idea?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Kitchen islands are super popular, and for good reason! They offer extra counter space, storage, and a casual dining spot. But here’s the thing: they aren't always the perfect fit for every kitchen layout.

Myth: An island is a must-have for every modern kitchen.

Fact: Not quite! Islands need enough clearance around them – at least 36-42 inches – to allow for comfortable movement and appliance access. In smaller kitchens, an island can actually make the space feel more cramped and hinder flow. Sometimes a peninsula or even a movable cart is a better solution for added functionality without sacrificing precious floor space. We help you figure out what makes sense for your specific kitchen, whether it's in Summerlin or Henderson.

Myth: Islands are only for cooking and prep.

Fact: Islands are incredibly versatile! They can house sinks, dishwashers, wine fridges, microwaves, and even serve as a dedicated homework station or a casual gathering spot for guests. The key is to design it with your lifestyle in mind. We've designed islands that are central hubs for families, not just another countertop.

Myth: Installing an island is a simple DIY job.

Fact: While a freestanding island might be, a built-in island often involves plumbing, electrical work, and structural considerations, especially if you want a cooktop or sink. This isn't just about placing a cabinet; it’s about integrating utilities safely and correctly, which requires a licensed general contractor. Getting it wrong can lead to costly repairs down the line. It's always best to have a professional assess your space and handle the installation to ensure it meets all Nevada requirements.
