Mistakes Homeowners Make During Kitchen Design

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Renovating your kitchen is a big undertaking, and it's easy to get caught up in the excitement. However, a few common missteps can really mess up your dream kitchen. Avoiding these can save you a lot of grief and money down the road.

  • Underestimating the Budget: This is a huge one. Many people forget to account for labor, permits, and unforeseen issues. Always add a 10-20% contingency fund to avoid mid-project financial stress. It’s better to have it and not need it than to need it and not have it.
  • Poor Layout Planning: Just because it looks good in a magazine doesn't mean it works for your life. Ignoring the work triangle (sink, stove, fridge), not having enough prep space, or creating awkward traffic flows will make your kitchen a pain to use every day. Think about how you actually cook and live.
  • Choosing Appliances Too Late: Your appliances dictate cabinet openings and clearances. Picking them after your design is locked in can lead to costly changes or ill-fitting units. Select your key appliances early on.
  • Inadequate Lighting and Ventilation: A beautiful kitchen is useless if you can't see what you're doing or if cooking smells linger for days. Plan for task lighting over counters and robust ventilation over your range. It makes a massive difference in usability and comfort.
  • Skipping Permits: For structural changes, new plumbing, or electrical work, permits are almost always required in Las Vegas. Skipping them can lead to forced corrections, insurance problems, and issues when you sell your home. A professional contractor like Simply Construction will manage this for you.
