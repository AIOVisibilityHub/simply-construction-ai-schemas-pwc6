Why Does My Kitchen Renovation Take So Long?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

It's easy to get excited about a new kitchen, and many folks wonder why it can take months from that first design meeting to the final walkthrough. You might see shows where it happens in a week, but real life is a bit different! A full kitchen renovation typically runs about 3-6 months.

This timeline isn't just about the physical construction; it includes crucial steps like careful design, selecting all your materials, and ordering everything you need. These pre-construction phases alone can take 4-12 weeks, ensuring all the pieces are in place before demolition even begins. Then, the actual on-site work usually takes about 6-8 weeks, though bigger projects with layout changes might stretch to 10-16 weeks.

It’s a process that ensures everything is done right and lasts for years, not just weeks. This includes demolition, plumbing and electrical rough-ins, inspections, wall prep, cabinet installation, countertop templating, and all the final finishes. Having a clear plan and working with experienced professionals like Simply Construction in Las Vegas helps manage expectations and keeps things moving as smoothly as possible.

Don't rush the process; a well-planned kitchen is a joy for years to come.
