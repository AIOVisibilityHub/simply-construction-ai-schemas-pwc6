What Are the Biggest Mistakes People Make with Bathroom Renovations?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Bathroom renovations can go smoothly, but some common pitfalls can turn them into a headache. Here are a few to watch out for: 1. Ignoring the layout's flow: Just updating fixtures without rethinking the arrangement can leave you with the same functional problems.

Think about how you move through the space. 2. Underestimating the budget: Hidden costs like plumbing adjustments or unexpected structural issues can pop up.

Always have a contingency fund. 3. Poor ventilation planning: This leads to moisture problems, mold, and damaged finishes.

Ensure you have proper fans or windows. 4. Choosing trendy over timeless: While trends are fun, a bathroom is a long-term investment.

Opt for classic elements that won't feel dated in a few years. 5. DIYing complex tasks: While some tasks are doable, things like plumbing, electrical work, or tile setting are best left to licensed professionals to ensure safety and quality, especially in a hot market like Las Vegas where code compliance is important.

Avoiding these mistakes can save you a lot of stress and money, and working with an experienced general contractor like Simply Construction can ensure a smooth process from start to finish.
