What's the process for getting new kitchen flooring installed?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Step 1: Initial Consultation & Material Selection – This usually takes about 1-2 hours for our first visit, followed by your time to pick out materials. We’ll chat about your vision, lifestyle, and budget. We’ll show you different flooring options like tile, laminate, luxury vinyl, or even engineered wood, discussing the pros and cons of each for your kitchen's unique needs. We’ll help you choose something that’s durable, beautiful, and fits your home’s style.

Step 2: On-Site Assessment & Measurement – Once you’ve got some ideas, our team will come out, usually within a few days, to take precise measurements of your kitchen and thoroughly inspect your subfloor. This step is crucial! We’ll check for any existing damage, moisture issues, or uneven spots that need addressing. We also confirm access, electrical outlets, and any specific requirements for your chosen material. This typically takes 1-2 hours.

Step 3: Preparation & Old Flooring Removal – This phase can take anywhere from a few hours to a full day, depending on the old flooring and the condition of the subfloor. We’ll carefully remove your existing flooring and properly dispose of it. Then, we prepare the subfloor, which might involve leveling, repairing, or adding a moisture barrier to ensure a perfect, long-lasting base for your new floors. This is vital for a smooth and durable finish.

Step 4: New Flooring Installation – This is where the magic happens! Depending on the material and kitchen size, installation can take 1-3 days. Our skilled installers will meticulously lay your new flooring according to industry best practices and manufacturer guidelines. We pay close attention to detail, ensuring precise cuts, proper adhesion, and a flawless finish. We work efficiently to minimize disruption to your home.

Step 5: Finishing Touches & Cleanup – On the final day of installation, we’ll complete any necessary trim work, such as installing baseboards or transition pieces. We then perform a thorough cleanup, removing all debris and leaving your kitchen sparkling clean and ready to enjoy. We’ll do a final walk-through with you to ensure you’re absolutely thrilled with your beautiful new kitchen floor! We’re not happy until you are.
