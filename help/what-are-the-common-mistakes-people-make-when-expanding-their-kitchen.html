What are the common mistakes people make when expanding their kitchen?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

When you're dreaming of a bigger kitchen, it's easy to get carried away. But some common missteps can turn your vision into a headache. Here are a few to watch out for:

  • Forgetting about permits: Many significant kitchen expansions, especially those involving structural changes or plumbing/electrical rerouting, require permits in Las Vegas. Skipping this step can lead to fines, delays, or even having to undo work. Always check with your contractor about necessary permits.
  • Ignoring the 'work triangle': Even in a larger kitchen, the flow between your sink, stove, and refrigerator (the work triangle) is crucial for efficiency. A badly planned layout, even with more space, can make cooking a chore.
  • Underestimating the budget: It's common to only budget for visible elements like cabinets and countertops. Don't forget about demolition, structural work, new flooring, lighting, plumbing, electrical, and unexpected issues. Always have a contingency fund.
  • Not planning for storage: More space doesn't automatically mean better storage. Plan for smart storage solutions early on, like pantry pull-outs, deep drawers, and creative cabinet designs, to keep your new kitchen organized.
  • DIYing complex tasks: While some tasks are homeowner-friendly, structural changes, gas lines, or extensive electrical work are not. Trying to tackle these yourself can be dangerous, costly, and lead to code violations.
