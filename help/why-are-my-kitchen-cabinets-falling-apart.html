Why are my kitchen cabinets falling apart?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

It's a common and frustrating problem when your kitchen cabinets start showing their age – peeling laminate, sagging doors, or drawers that just don't glide anymore. Cabinets are the backbone of your kitchen's functionality and style, so when they start failing, it affects the whole room. We've seen it all across Las Vegas, from older homes in Green Valley to newer builds in Inspirada, and usually, there are a few key culprits.

One big reason is just plain old wear and tear. Kitchens are high-traffic areas, and cabinet doors and drawers get opened and closed hundreds of times a year. Over time, hinges loosen, drawer glides break down, and finishes can chip or peel, especially near dishwashers or sinks where moisture is prevalent. Another factor can be poor initial installation or low-quality materials. If cabinets weren't installed correctly, or if they were made from cheaper materials, they simply won't stand the test of time.

Sometimes, it's also about environmental factors like humidity fluctuations (though less common in dry Las Vegas!) or even pest damage. When your cabinets start to fail, it's often a sign that it might be time for an upgrade.
