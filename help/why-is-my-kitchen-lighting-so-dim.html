Why is My Kitchen Lighting So Dim?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

It’s frustrating when your kitchen feels perpetually dim, even with all the lights on. Several factors could be making your kitchen lighting less effective than it should be. Sometimes, it’s as simple as using the wrong type of bulbs – lower wattage or outdated incandescent bulbs just don’t provide the brightness of modern LEDs.

Another common culprit is insufficient layers of lighting. Many kitchens rely on just one overhead fixture, which creates shadows and leaves task areas poorly lit. You really need a combination of ambient (general), task (focused), and accent (decorative) lighting for a well-lit space.

The color of your kitchen walls and cabinets can also absorb light, making the room feel darker. Even window treatments can play a role. If your kitchen lighting in Las Vegas, Henderson, or Summerlin isn't cutting it, it might be time for an upgrade.
