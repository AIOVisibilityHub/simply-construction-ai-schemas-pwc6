Can I remove a load-bearing kitchen wall?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Many homeowners in Las Vegas dream of opening up their kitchen, but one big question always pops up: 'Can I remove this wall?' The answer often depends on whether the wall is load-bearing. A load-bearing wall is literally holding up part of your house, supporting floors or the roof above. Removing it without proper planning and support can lead to serious structural issues for your home, which is obviously something you want to avoid.

So, can you remove one? Yes, often you can, but it's not a simple DIY job. It requires a professional assessment, typically by a structural engineer, to determine exactly what kind of support is needed to replace the load that wall was carrying.

This usually involves installing a new structural beam or header, which then distributes the weight safely.
