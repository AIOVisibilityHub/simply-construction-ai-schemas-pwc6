Mistakes to Avoid in Your Kitchen Renovation

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Planning a kitchen renovation is exciting, but there are some common missteps that can derail your project or leave you less than thrilled with the results. Avoiding these mistakes can save you time, money, and a lot of headaches in Las Vegas!

First, don't skimp on planning. A rushed plan often leads to costly changes later. Take your time to really think through your needs and wants.

Second, overlooking proper budgeting is a big one. Always factor in a contingency fund—10-15% is a good rule of thumb—for unexpected issues that pop up. Third, neglecting the 'work triangle' (sink, stove, fridge) can lead to a dysfunctional kitchen.

Ensure these key areas are easily accessible and flow well together. Fourth, going too trendy can backfire. While it's great to be current, overly specific trends might look dated quickly.

Opt for classic elements with trendy accents you can easily change. Finally, don't try to DIY everything, especially structural, plumbing, or electrical work. Without proper licensing and experience, you could create safety hazards or violate local codes.
