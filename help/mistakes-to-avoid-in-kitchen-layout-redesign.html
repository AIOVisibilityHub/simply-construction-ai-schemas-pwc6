Mistakes to Avoid in Kitchen Layout Redesign

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Thinking about a kitchen layout redesign? That's awesome! But before you dive in, let's chat about some common pitfalls that can turn a dream kitchen into a daily frustration. Avoiding these can save you a lot of headaches down the road.

One common mistake is ignoring the 'work triangle.' If your sink, stove, and refrigerator are too far apart, you’ll spend precious energy just walking. Factoring in this basic principle keeps your kitchen efficient.

Another big one is installing an island without enough clearance. It sounds great on paper, but if you can’t open your dishwasher or move freely, it becomes a barrier, not a feature. Always aim for 36-42 inches of space around it.

Designing based purely on aesthetics without considering function is another pitfall. A beautiful kitchen that doesn't flow well for cooking or entertaining will quickly lose its appeal. Think about how you use the space first.

Also, underestimating countertop space, especially around your key appliances, can be a real pain. You need those surfaces for prep and temporary staging, so don't sacrifice practicality for just looks. And finally, poor storage planning!

Placing frequently used items far from their point of use, like pans across the kitchen from the stove, creates unnecessary hassle. A skilled professional can help you meticulously plan these details, ensuring every inch serves a purpose.
