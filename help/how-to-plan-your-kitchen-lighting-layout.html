How to Plan Your Kitchen Lighting Layout

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Planning your kitchen lighting layout is a bit like designing a custom wardrobe for your home—you want layers for every occasion! The key is to think about different types of light and where you need them most. Start with ambient lighting; this is your general, overhead illumination, usually from recessed lights or a stylish ceiling fixture.

It's the baseline for your kitchen's brightness. Next, consider task lighting. This is super important for areas where you're chopping veggies, reading recipes, or doing homework.

Under-cabinet lighting is fantastic for this, shining directly on your countertops without shadows. Over-sink lights are also a must. Then, there's accent lighting, which is all about highlighting architectural features or decorative elements, like a beautiful backsplash or shelving.

Picture small puck lights inside glass-front cabinets or directional lights on a piece of art. Finally, don't forget decorative lighting, like pendant lights over an island or a striking chandelier in an open-concept kitchen. These add personality and charm.
