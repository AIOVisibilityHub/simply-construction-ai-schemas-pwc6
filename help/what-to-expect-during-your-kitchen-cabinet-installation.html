What to Expect During Your Kitchen Cabinet Installation?

Step 1: Preparation (typically 1-2 days). This initial phase involves clearing out the kitchen area, protecting your floors and surrounding spaces, and ensuring the walls are ready. If there's demolition of old cabinets, that happens here. The installers will also double-check the measurements and plans to avoid any surprises. This step is crucial for a smooth installation.

Step 2: Base Cabinet Installation (typically 1-3 days). The base cabinets are usually installed first, as they provide the foundation for your countertops and often dictate the placement of other elements like appliances. Our team will carefully level and secure each cabinet, ensuring they're perfectly aligned. Precision here is key for a seamless look and proper functionality.

Step 3: Upper Cabinet Installation (typically 1-2 days). Once the base cabinets are in place, the upper cabinets go in. This requires careful measurement and often two installers to lift and secure them properly to the wall studs. They'll be leveled and aligned with each other and the base cabinets, ensuring a cohesive and sturdy installation. This can be a bit tricky, especially with custom or unusually sized units.

Step 4: Filler Panels and Trim (typically 1 day). After the main cabinet boxes are installed, our team will add any necessary filler panels to bridge gaps between cabinets and walls, and install crown molding or other decorative trim pieces. These details are what give your kitchen a finished, custom look. It's all about those perfect lines and tight seams.
