What challenges should I expect with a kitchen expansion?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Embarking on a kitchen expansion project is exciting, but it's smart to know what challenges might pop up so you can be prepared. Think of it as a roadmap for a smoother journey! First, dealing with existing structural elements can be a big one.

If you're looking to remove a wall, especially a load-bearing one, you'll need expert assessment and structural beam installation. This isn't a DIY job, and getting it wrong can cause serious issues. Second, permitting and inspections are crucial.

Nevada has specific requirements, and navigating these can be complex. A professional general contractor, like Simply Construction, will handle all the necessary planning and permits to ensure everything is up to code and safe. Third, the coordination of trades is a dance.

Electricians, plumbers, cabinet installers – they all need to work together seamlessly. Any misstep can cause delays and cost overruns. That's where experienced project management comes in handy.

Finally, living through a renovation can be disruptive. Planning for a temporary kitchen setup and understanding the timeline helps manage expectations. Our team at Simply Construction has over 30 years of experience coordinating these complex projects in areas like Henderson and Spring Valley, so we've seen it all.
