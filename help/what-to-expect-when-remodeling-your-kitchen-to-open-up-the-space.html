What to Expect When Remodeling Your Kitchen to Open Up the Space

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Step 1: Initial Consultation and Design. This is where we discuss your vision, how you use your kitchen, and what you hope to achieve. We'll look at your home’s existing layout and assess the feasibility of wall removal, considering structural elements. This phase usually takes a week or two, involving measurements and preliminary design ideas.

Step 2: Structural Assessment and Engineering. If a wall needs to come down, especially a load-bearing one, an engineer will assess it to determine the best approach for structural integrity. This ensures your home remains safe and sound. This critical step can take a few weeks as reports are generated and reviewed.

Step 3: Permitting and Planning. Based on the engineering assessment and design, we'll draw up detailed plans and submit them to the local Las Vegas authorities for permits. This stage can vary greatly but often takes several weeks to a few months, as we navigate local building codes and regulations.

Step 4: Demolition and Framing. Once permits are secured, the fun begins! We carefully demolish the designated walls and install any necessary structural supports, like new beams. This part is usually quite quick, often completed within a week or two, depending on the complexity.

Step 5: Rough-Ins (Electrical, Plumbing, HVAC). With the new layout defined, our skilled tradespeople will rough in new electrical wiring, plumbing lines, and HVAC ducts as needed for your new kitchen configuration. This phase can take a couple of weeks.

Step 6: Finishing Touches. This is where your kitchen truly starts to take shape – cabinets are installed, countertops are fitted, flooring is laid, and appliances are hooked up. Expect this phase to be the longest, potentially several weeks, as each element is carefully installed and finished.

Step 7: Final Inspection and Walkthrough. After all the work is done, local inspectors will sign off on the project. We’ll then do a final walkthrough with you to ensure everything meets your expectations and our high standards. This is typically a day or two.
