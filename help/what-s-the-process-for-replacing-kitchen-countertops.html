What’s the Process for Replacing Kitchen Countertops?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Step 1: Initial Consultation & Design. This is where we chat about your vision! We'll visit your home in places like Henderson or Paradise, discuss your style preferences, material choices (granite, quartz, etc.), budget, and any functional needs. We’ll take initial measurements and help you narrow down the best options for your kitchen. This stage is all about making sure we’re on the same page.

Step 2: Detailed Measurements & Fabrication. Once you've picked your perfect material, our team will come back for precise, laser-accurate measurements. This is super important to ensure a perfect fit. After measuring, your chosen countertop material is sent to be fabricated. This custom process takes some time as the material is cut, shaped, and polished according to your kitchen's exact dimensions and your chosen edge profiles.

Step 3: Old Countertop Removal. Before the new ones go in, the old countertops need to come out. Our crew carefully removes your existing counters, ensuring minimal disruption to your kitchen. We’ll also disconnect your sink and any cooktops. We handle the demolition and disposal, so you don’t have to worry about the mess.

Step 4: Installation of New Countertops. This is the exciting part! Our skilled installers bring in your beautiful new countertops. They’ll carefully set them in place, secure them, and ensure all seams are meticulously aligned and blended. We'll also reinstall your sink and connect the plumbing, along with any cooktops. We make sure everything is level, secure, and looking absolutely stunning.

Step 5: Final Walkthrough & Clean-up. Once the installation is complete, we’ll do a thorough clean-up of the work area. We’ll then walk through the project with you to ensure every detail meets your satisfaction. Your new countertops will be ready to enjoy! We handle projects like these across the Las Vegas Valley, from Summerlin to Spring Valley, making the process as stress-free as possible.
