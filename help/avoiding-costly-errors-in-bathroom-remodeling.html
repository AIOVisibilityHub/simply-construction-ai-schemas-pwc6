Avoiding Costly Errors in Bathroom Remodeling

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

When you're diving into a bathroom remodel, a few common mistakes can turn your dream into a headache. Here's what to look out for and how to steer clear:

  • Starting without confirming permit requirements — how to avoid it: Always check with your contractor about local permit requirements for plumbing, electrical, or structural changes. Moving forward without them can lead to stop-work orders and costly delays.
  • Inadequate or incorrect waterproofing — how to avoid it: This is critical, especially in showers and wet zones. Don't rely solely on cement backer board; ensure a dedicated waterproofing membrane is properly installed according to manufacturer instructions. Faulty waterproofing leads to leaks and hidden damage.
  • Poor ventilation planning — how to avoid it: An undersized or missing exhaust fan leads to moisture buildup, mold risk, and material degradation. Plan for proper ventilation from the start.
  • Sequencing errors in the build — how to avoid it: Tiling before inspections are passed or before waterproofing cures can force rework. A good contractor follows a logical sequence, ensuring each step is correctly completed before moving to the next.
  • Not measuring fixture rough-ins or door swings — how to avoid it: Simple layout mistakes like miscalculating a toilet rough-in or forgetting about door swings can cause major headaches. Double-check all measurements and consider how the space will function.
  • Accepting vague contractor estimates — how to avoid it: An estimate that lacks defined scope, order of operations, and allowances is a recipe for cost overruns and disputes. Insist on a detailed written scope and clear pricing.

By keeping these in mind, you can help ensure your bathroom remodel in Las Vegas goes smoothly and beautifully.
