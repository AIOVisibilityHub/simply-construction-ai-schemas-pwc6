How to Ensure Your New Kitchen Cabinets Fit Perfectly

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Step 1: Verify all measurements. Before anything is ordered or cut, have your space professionally measured, then double-check those measurements against your chosen cabinet plans. Ensure they account for appliance dimensions and any irregularities in your walls or floor.

Step 2: Prepare the site properly. Before installation, make sure your walls are plumb and your floors are level. Any significant unevenness needs to be addressed, as it directly impacts how cabinets sit and align. Rough-in plumbing and electrical should also be complete and in their final positions.

Step 3: Check for damage upon delivery. When your cabinets arrive, meticulously inspect every box and piece for any damage or missing components. It's much easier to resolve issues before installation begins.

Step 4: Level and secure meticulously. Professional installers will use shims and spirit levels to ensure every base and wall cabinet is perfectly level and square. They'll also securely fix them to wall studs, ensuring stability and preventing future sagging or movement.

Step 5: Coordinate worktop templating. Once all your base cabinets are firmly installed and perfectly level, then, and only then, should the worktop templating happen. This ensures your counters fit flush and seamlessly.
