Mistakes to Avoid When Choosing Kitchen Countertops

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Picking out new kitchen countertops is a big decision, and it’s super exciting, but it’s also easy to get tripped up if you’re not careful. Here are some common blunders folks make and how you can sidestep them to ensure you love your new surfaces:

  • Ignoring Your Lifestyle: Don't just pick something because it looks good. If you've got a busy family, cook a lot, or entertain often, you need durable, easy-to-clean materials like quartz or a tough granite. Delicate options might not hold up, leading to early regrets.
  • Forgetting the Sink Cut-Out: This sounds minor, but it's a critical detail! Your sink type (undermount, drop-in, farmhouse) dictates the countertop fabrication. Make sure your contractor knows your sink choice early on to avoid delays or extra costs for re-cutting.
  • Not Budgeting for the Whole Picture: The cost per square foot for the material is just one part. Remember to factor in demolition, fabrication, installation, and any new plumbing connections. A professional contractor like Simply Construction provides a clear, comprehensive estimate so there are no surprises.
  • Choosing Samples in the Wrong Lighting: That beautiful slab looks amazing in the showroom, but how does it look in your kitchen's natural and artificial light? Always take samples home and view them at different times of day to get a real feel for the color and tone.
  • Overlooking the Sealing and Maintenance Needs: Some natural stones require regular sealing to protect against stains, while engineered materials are virtually maintenance-free. Make sure you understand the upkeep involved for your chosen material before committing, so you don't end up with more chores than you bargained for.
