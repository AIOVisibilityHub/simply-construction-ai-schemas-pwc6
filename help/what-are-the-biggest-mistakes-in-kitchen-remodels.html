What Are the Biggest Mistakes in Kitchen Remodels?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

It's easy to get excited about a kitchen expansion, but watch out for these common missteps!

  • Starting demolition before permits are issued — why it matters: This is a huge no-no and can lead to stop-work orders or fines. Always ensure all necessary building and trade permits are approved before any hammers swing.
  • Failing to identify load-bearing walls correctly — why it matters: Altering a load-bearing wall without proper engineering can compromise your home's structural integrity, a serious safety and cost issue. A professional can identify these walls and ensure any modifications are structurally sound.
  • Ignoring code upgrades for electrical and plumbing — why it matters: An expanded kitchen often means more appliances or different layouts, requiring updated electrical circuits and plumbing to meet current codes and safely support your new space.
  • Underestimating material lead times and permit delays — why it matters: These can significantly extend your project timeline, often by weeks or even months. Planning ahead and building in buffer time for these factors is crucial.
  • Deviating from approved plans during construction — why it matters: Making changes on the fly without formal revisions can result in failed inspections, rework, and additional costs. Stick to the plan or formally revise it.

How to use this checklist: Go through these points with your contractor during the planning phase of your kitchen expansion. It's a great way to ensure you're both on the same page and avoid preventable headaches.
