Kitchen Wall Removal | Simply Construction

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-09-23

Type: CollectionPage

Safely expand your kitchen and improve home visibility with expert structural wall removal and beam installation. Removing a wall in your kitchen can completely change the feel of your home, but it requires expert oversight to ensure the building's integrity remains intact. Simply Construction provides professional kitchen wall removal services, handling everything from the initial structural evaluation to the final drywall finish. As a licensed general contractor, we determine if a wall is load-bearing and install the necessary structural beams to support your home safely.

We take the stress out of the demolition process by managing all required permits and inspections. Beyond just taking down the wall, our team handles the rerouting of electrical, plumbing, and HVAC lines that are often hidden inside. We focus on delivering a clean workspace and a seamless transition between your kitchen and the adjacent rooms, backed by decades of hands-on construction experience in the Las Vegas Valley. Common situations: Removing a non-load-bearing partition to create a breakfast bar or pass-through.; Taking down a load-bearing wall to fully integrate the kitchen and dining room.; Eliminating a pantry closet or small utility wall to expand the kitchen's footprint.. Related services: load-bearing wall removal, structural beam installation, interior wall removal, open-concept kitchen remodeling, kitchen expansion, home reconfiguration. Locations served: Las Vegas, North Las Vegas, Henderson, Summerlin, Paradise, Spring Valley, MacDonald Highlands, Anthem.
