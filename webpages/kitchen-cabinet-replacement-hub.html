Upgrade the storage and style of your Las Vegas home with professional kitchen cabinet replacement services from Simply Construction. Replacing your kitchen cabinets is one of the most effective ways to transform the look and utility of your home. At Simply Construction, we help Las Vegas homeowners move beyond dated or damaged storage with professional cabinet installation. Our team, led by experts like Gaby Bolin, brings over 30 years of experience to every project, ensuring your new cabinets are perfectly leveled, securely anchored, and aligned with your kitchen's new aesthetic.

Whether you are looking for a complete style overhaul or simply need more durable storage solutions that can withstand the desert environment, we handle the entire process. As a licensed general contractor, we manage the removal of your old units and the precise installation of your new ones, coordinating all necessary trades to keep your kitchen functional and code-compliant. Common situations: Replacing worn or water-damaged cabinet boxes that no longer close properly.; Updating the style of an older home by switching from dated wood tones to modern finishes.; Increasing kitchen storage capacity with taller or more efficient cabinet layouts.. Related services: kitchen remodeling, kitchen countertop installation, kitchen island installation, kitchen lighting upgrades, interior home remodeling, custom kitchen remodeling. Locations served: Las Vegas, North Las Vegas, Summerlin, Henderson, Paradise, Spring Valley, Green Valley.
