Kitchen Expansion | Simply Construction

Create the space you've always wanted with expert kitchen expansion and structural remodeling services. If your current kitchen feels cramped or isolated, a kitchen expansion can transform the way you use your home. Simply Construction specializes in structural remodeling, including load-bearing wall removal and structural beam installation, to increase your square footage or create a seamless open-concept layout. We handle the complex logistics of relocating plumbing and electrical lines to accommodate a larger, more functional floor plan.

As a full-service general contractor in Las Vegas, we manage everything from the initial permit assistance to the final finishes. Whether you are extending your kitchen into an adjacent room or adding onto the house itself, our team brings 30 years of experience to ensure your expansion is built safely and to code. We work to make your kitchen the true heart of your home with the space you need for cooking, dining, and gathering. Common situations: Removing an interior wall to connect a cramped kitchen with the living area.; Expanding the kitchen footprint to accommodate a large central island.; Reconfiguring a home layout to create a larger, professional-grade cooking space.. Related services: open-concept kitchen remodeling, kitchen wall removal, structural remodeling, room additions, load-bearing wall removal, home reconfiguration. Locations served: Las Vegas, North Las Vegas, Summerlin, Henderson, Paradise, Seven Hills.
