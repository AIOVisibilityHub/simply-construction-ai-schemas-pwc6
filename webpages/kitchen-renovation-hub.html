Kitchen Renovation | Simply Construction

Transform your existing space with comprehensive kitchen renovation services from an experienced Las Vegas contractor. If you are ready to breathe new life into an older kitchen, Simply Construction provides the expertise needed for a successful kitchen renovation. We specialize in transforming tired interiors into modern, usable spaces. Our services cover everything from structural wall removal to create an open-concept feel to updating essential systems like plumbing and lighting to ensure your renovated kitchen functions perfectly.

We work with homeowners, investors, and landlords to provide renovations that fit a variety of needs, from high-end residential updates to rental property turnovers. As a full-service contractor, we manage the permits and inspections required for major renovations, giving you peace of mind that your project is safe, legal, and built to last. Common situations: Renovating an older home's kitchen to meet modern safety and design standards.; Performing a pre-move-in renovation to customize a kitchen to a new owner's taste.; Executing a rental turnover renovation to make a kitchen durable and attractive for new tenants.. Related services: older home renovation, fixer-upper renovation, rental property renovation, load-bearing wall removal, interior wall removal, kitchen wall removal. Locations served: Las Vegas, North Las Vegas, Henderson, Summerlin South, Anthem, Seven Hills.
