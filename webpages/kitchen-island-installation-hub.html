Kitchen Island Installation | Simply Construction

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-09-23

Type: CollectionPage

Add value and utility to your home with a custom kitchen island designed and installed by Simply Construction. A kitchen island is more than just extra counter space; it is often the hub of the home for meal prep, dining, and socializing. Simply Construction designs and installs custom kitchen islands that improve the flow and functionality of your floor plan. Whether you want a simple prep station or a large island with integrated appliances and seating, our team handles the structural and utility requirements.

Adding an island often involves bringing electrical power or plumbing to the center of the room. As licensed contractors with 30 years of experience, we manage all necessary permits and inspections for these upgrades. From load-bearing considerations to flooring transitions, we ensure your new island looks like an original, seamless part of your kitchen. Common situations: Adding a central workstation to a large, open-concept kitchen layout.; Installing a multi-functional island with a built-in sink, dishwasher, or microwave.; Creating a breakfast bar or casual dining area for family and guests.. Related services: open-concept kitchen remodeling, kitchen layout redesign, kitchen cabinet installation, kitchen countertop installation, structural remodeling, load-bearing wall removal. Locations served: Las Vegas, Summerlin, Henderson, North Las Vegas, Southern Highlands, MacDonald Highlands.
