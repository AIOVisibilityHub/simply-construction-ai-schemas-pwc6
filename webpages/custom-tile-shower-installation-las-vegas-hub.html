Custom Tile Shower Installation | Simply Construction

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-09-21

Type: CollectionPage

Custom tile shower design and construction in Las Vegas, featuring professional waterproofing, masonry, and high-quality finishes. A custom tile shower is a centerpiece for any modern bathroom renovation. Simply Construction specializes in designing and building custom tile showers that combine aesthetic appeal with long-term durability. Our team handles every step of the construction process, from demolition and waterproofing to precise tile layout and final finishes. With over 30 years of hands-on experience in Las Vegas, we ensure your shower is built to Nevada state codes.

Whether you are interested in a walk-in shower design or a complete tub-to-shower conversion, we use high-quality materials designed to withstand the rigors of daily use. We manage all trade scheduling, including plumbing and electrical work, providing a seamless experience for homeowners in Henderson, Summerlin, and throughout the valley. Common situations: Converting an old bathtub into a modern, custom walk-in tile shower.; Designing a luxury primary suite with a large-format tile shower.; Replacing a leaking or moldy fiberglass shower insert with custom masonry.; Upgrading a guest bathroom with a contemporary tiled shower design.. Related services: bathroom remodeling, walk-in shower installation, tub-to-shower conversion, bathroom tile installation, custom tile work, home renovation. Locations served: Las Vegas, North Las Vegas, Summerlin, Henderson, Paradise, Spring Valley, Green Valley.
