Kitchen Layout Redesign | Simply Construction

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-09-21

Type: CollectionPage

Optimize your kitchen's workflow and storage with a professional layout redesign from a licensed general contractor. A successful kitchen remodel starts with a layout that maximizes efficiency and fits your lifestyle. Simply Construction offers kitchen layout redesign services to help homeowners move beyond the limitations of their current space. Whether you want to relocate your sink to a new island, move the stove for better workflow, or expand the entire kitchen footprint, our team coordinates the complex plumbing and electrical changes needed to make it happen.

With more than 30 years of experience, we understand the technical requirements of Nevada building codes. We manage every phase of the redesign, from permitting and demolition to the final installation of cabinets and countertops. Our goal is to create a functional, modern workspace that eliminates bottlenecks and makes cooking and entertaining easier for you and your family. Common situations: Converting a traditional U-shaped kitchen into an open L-shape with a central island.; Relocating major appliances to create a more ergonomic 'work triangle.'; Reconfiguring a small, dated kitchen to accommodate modern, full-sized appliances.. Related services: kitchen remodeling, kitchen expansion, kitchen island installation, custom kitchen remodeling, floor plan redesign, kitchen cabinet installation. Locations served: Las Vegas, North Las Vegas, Henderson, Summerlin, Paradise, Spring Valley, Southern Highlands, The Lakes.
