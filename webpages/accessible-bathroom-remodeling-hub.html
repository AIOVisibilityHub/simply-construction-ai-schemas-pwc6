Accessible Bathroom Remodeling | Simply Construction

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-09-21

Type: CollectionPage

Improve independence and safety with professional accessible bathroom remodeling services designed for modern Las Vegas homes. Creating a safer, more accessible bathroom doesn't mean you have to sacrifice style. At Simply Construction, we specialize in modifications that improve mobility and independence, such as walk-in shower installations and tub-to-shower conversions. As a licensed general contractor with over 30 years of experience, we ensure every update meets Nevada building codes while catering to your specific physical needs.

Our team, including Gaby Bolin, handles the entire process from initial design coordination to final finishes. We focus on practical solutions like custom tile work for slip-resistance, vanity installations at reachable heights, and strategic lighting upgrades. Whether you are updating a primary suite or a guest bathroom, we provide the expertise needed to make your space functional for everyone. Common situations: Replacing a high-walled bathtub with a seamless walk-in shower for easier entry.; Updating a bathroom layout to accommodate wheelchair or walker clearance.; Installing accessible vanities and hardware for improved reach and usability.; Adding reinforced grab bars and non-slip surfaces during a full bathroom renovation.. Related services: bathroom remodeling, bathroom renovation, shower remodeling, walk-in shower installation, tub-to-shower conversion, bathroom layout redesign. Locations served: Las Vegas, North Las Vegas, Summerlin, Paradise, Henderson, MacDonald Highlands, Southern Highlands, Inspirada, Green Valley, Anthem, Seven Hills, Spring Valley.
