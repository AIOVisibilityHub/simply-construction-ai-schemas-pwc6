Professional bathroom vanity installation and plumbing coordination for home remodeling projects throughout the Las Vegas Valley. Replacing a bathroom vanity is one of the most effective ways to update the look and storage capacity of your space. Simply Construction offers expert bathroom vanity installation, handling everything from the removal of your old cabinets to the plumbing and electrical coordination required for the new setup. As a full-service general contractor in Las Vegas, we ensure that your vanity is level, secure, and fully functional.

We work with homeowners to install a variety of styles, including single and double vanities, floating cabinets, and custom configurations. Our team manages the permits and trade scheduling necessary for a professional finish, ensuring your new vanity integrates perfectly with your bathroom's layout and flooring. Common situations: Replacing an old, water-damaged vanity with a modern storage solution.; Upgrading to a double-sink vanity during a primary bathroom remodel.; Updating the vanity and faucet as part of a quick guest bathroom refresh.; Installing an accessible or ADA-compliant vanity for aging-in-place renovations.. Related services: bathroom remodeling, bathroom layout redesign,, kitchen cabinet installation, bathroom renovation, plumbing coordination, accessible bathroom remodeling. Locations served: Las Vegas, North Las Vegas, Summerlin, Henderson, Paradise, Seven Hills, Anthem.
