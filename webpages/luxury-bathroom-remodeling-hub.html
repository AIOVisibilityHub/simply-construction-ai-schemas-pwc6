Achieve a high-end look and feel with luxury bathroom remodeling services from Simply Construction, serving Las Vegas's most prestigious communities. Elevate your home with a luxury bathroom remodel that combines high-end aesthetics with expert craftsmanship. Simply Construction provides premium renovation services for luxury properties throughout Las Vegas, Summerlin, and Henderson. We specialize in custom tile shower installations, high-end vanity replacements, and sophisticated lighting upgrades designed to create a spa-like atmosphere in your own home.

Our team brings more than 30 years of experience to every high-end project, managing complex layout redesigns and structural modifications with precision. We handle all permits and code compliance, ensuring that your luxury upgrades—from custom stonework to modern plumbing fixtures—are installed to the highest standards. Whether you are updating a primary suite in MacDonald Highlands or refreshing a custom home in Seven Hills, we deliver clean, durable, and sophisticated results. Common situations: Designing and building a custom spa-style walk-in shower with premium tile.; Installing high-end finishes, custom vanities, and designer lighting fixtures.; Redesigning a bathroom layout to accommodate a freestanding tub and open floor plan.; Upgrading a bathroom with smart features and luxury materials for a modern aesthetic.. Related services: bathroom remodeling, custom tile shower installation, bathroom layout redesign, primary suite addition, home modernization, interior home remodeling. Locations served: Las Vegas, Summerlin, Henderson, MacDonald Highlands, Seven Hills, Anthem, Southern Highlands.
