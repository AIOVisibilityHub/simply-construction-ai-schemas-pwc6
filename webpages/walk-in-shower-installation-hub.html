Walk-In Shower Installation | Simply Construction

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-09-21

Type: CollectionPage

Professional walk-in shower installation for a modern, accessible, and high-end bathroom experience. A walk-in shower adds both style and accessibility to any Las Vegas home. Simply Construction designs and installs custom walk-in showers that eliminate high curbs and create a more open feel in the bathroom. As a licensed general contractor, we manage the critical technical aspects of these installs, including proper floor sloping for drainage and advanced waterproofing to prevent future leaks.

Whatever your aesthetic—from modern floor-to-ceiling glass to intricate custom tile—our team ensures the final product is a seamless addition to your home. We serve residential and multi-family properties, providing professional installations that prioritize safety, ease of entry, and long-term durability. Common situations: Replacing a traditional shower with a modern, curbless walk-in design.; Installing a spacious walk-in shower as part of a primary suite expansion.; Creating a more accessible bathing area for residents with mobility concerns.. Related services: bathroom remodeling, shower remodeling, custom tile shower installation, accessible bathroom remodeling, aging-in-place bathroom remodeling, tub-to-shower conversion. Locations served: Las Vegas, Henderson, Summerlin, North Las Vegas, Paradise, Spring Valley.
