Kitchen Countertop Installation | Simply Construction

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-09-23

Type: CollectionPage

Enhance your kitchen's workspace with professional countertop installation services by the experts at Simply Construction. The right countertop is the centerpiece of a functional kitchen. Simply Construction provides professional installation for a variety of durable materials designed to handle the daily demands of a busy household. From heat-resistant surfaces to low-maintenance stone, we ensure your new countertops are measured accurately and installed with seamless precision.

Our team manages the entire installation phase, including the necessary plumbing coordination for sinks and faucets. With over three decades of experience in the Las Vegas Valley, we understand how to work with materials that stand up to local conditions, providing you with a beautiful, long-lasting workspace that meets all Nevada building standards. Common situations: Installing new countertops as part of a complete kitchen renovation project.; Upgrading to heat-resistant materials like quartz or granite for better durability.; Matching new surfaces to a recently installed kitchen island or cabinetry set.. Related services: kitchen remodeling, kitchen countertop replacement, kitchen island installation, kitchen cabinet installation, custom kitchen remodeling, kitchen backsplash installation. Locations served: Las Vegas, Henderson, Summerlin, North Las Vegas, Paradise, Enterprise.
