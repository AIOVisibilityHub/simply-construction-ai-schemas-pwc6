Bathroom Tile Installation | Simply Construction

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-09-23

Type: CollectionPage

Professional bathroom tile installation for floors and walls, delivering clean and durable finishes for Las Vegas residential and commercial properties. Quality tile work is essential for a bathroom that is both beautiful and easy to maintain. Simply Construction provides professional bathroom tile installation for floors, shower walls, and backsplashes across the Las Vegas area. Our team is experienced in working with a wide variety of materials, ensuring each project is completed with the clean lines and durable finishes our clients expect from a licensed contractor with 30 years of experience.

We understand the unique challenges of building in the desert, which is why we focus on proper substrate preparation and code-compliant installation. Whether you are updating the flooring in a small powder room or installing intricate tile patterns in a primary bathroom, we manage the project from demolition to the final grout application. Common situations: Installing slip-resistant tile flooring during a bathroom renovation.; Updating a bathroom backsplash to match new countertops or vanities.; Tiling shower walls as part of a complete bathroom remodel.; Replacing cracked or outdated floor tiles in a high-traffic guest bathroom.. Related services: bathroom remodeling, custom tile shower installation, bathroom renovation, flooring installation, kitchen backsplash installation, interior home remodeling. Locations served: Las Vegas, North Las Vegas, Summerlin, Henderson, Paradise, Spring Valley, Enterprise.
