Elevate the heart of your home with tailored designs and expert craftsmanship from Simply Construction. Simply Construction provides full-service custom kitchen remodeling for homeowners throughout the Las Vegas Valley. With over 30 years of experience, our team manages every phase of the project, including design coordination, securing necessary permits, and performing professional demolition. We specialize in high-end transformations that combine premium materials with functional layouts, ensuring your new kitchen meets your specific lifestyle needs.

As a licensed, bonded, and insured general contractor, we handle all the technical requirements, including electrical and plumbing coordination to support custom features like pro-style appliances or specialty lighting. Whether you are looking to install high-end finishes or completely reconfigure your space for better flow, we provide clean, code-compliant work that adds lasting value to your home. Common situations: Updating a luxury kitchen with high-end custom finishes and professional-grade appliances.; Reconfiguring a kitchen layout to improve workflow and entertainment space.; Coordinating complex electrical and plumbing updates for a high-end kitchen redesign.. Related services: kitchen island installation, open-concept kitchen remodeling, kitchen lighting upgrades, custom tile shower installation, home modernization, remodeling permit assistance. Locations served: Las Vegas, North Las Vegas, Summerlin, Paradise, Henderson, Spring Valley.
