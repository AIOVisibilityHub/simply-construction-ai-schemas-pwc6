Tub-to-Shower Conversion | Simply Construction

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-09-21

Type: CollectionPage

Seamless tub-to-shower conversions that improve bathroom safety and maximize your available space. Converting an unused bathtub into a functional walk-in shower is one of the most effective ways to modernize a bathroom. Simply Construction has over 30 years of experience managing these specific conversions in the Las Vegas Valley. We handle the full process: removing the old tub, reconfiguring the plumbing for a shower drain, and installing a custom-tiled shower base and surround.

Our tub-to-shower conversions are ideal for homeowners looking to reclaim floor space or improve safety. Because we are a full-service general contractor, we manage all necessary permits and inspections, ensuring the new plumbing and structural work meet Nevada state codes. Let Gaby Bolin and our team help you transition to a more practical and contemporary bathing solution. Common situations: Removing an old, seldom-used alcove tub to make room for a large shower.; Modifying a bathroom for better safety and easier entry.; Modernizing a guest bathroom during a whole-home renovation.. Related services: bathroom remodeling, shower remodeling, walk-in shower installation, accessible bathroom remodeling, aging-in-place bathroom remodeling, bathroom layout redesign. Locations served: Las Vegas, North Las Vegas, Henderson, Summerlin, Paradise, Spring Valley, Enterprise.
