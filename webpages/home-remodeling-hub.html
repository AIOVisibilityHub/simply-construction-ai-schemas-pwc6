Comprehensive residential remodeling services in Las Vegas, from interior updates to structural reconfigurations. Simply Construction is a full-service general contractor dedicated to transforming houses into ideal living spaces. Whether you are looking for a kitchen layout redesign, a modern bathroom renovation, or a complete interior home remodel, we bring more than 30 years of hands-on experience to every project. We serve homeowners across the Las Vegas Valley, managing everything from demolition and permitting to final inspections and finishes.

Our team handles the complexities of structural remodeling, including load-bearing wall removal to create open-concept floor plans. We work closely with you to modernize older homes, update fixer-uppers, or complete pre-move-in renovations. By coordinating all trades and ensuring code compliance, we take the stress out of home improvement, delivering high-quality results that enhance your property's value and utility. Common situations: Modernizing an older home with updated flooring, lighting, and interior finishes.; Removing interior walls to create an open-concept living and kitchen area.; Renovating a recently purchased fixer-upper before moving in.; Coordinating a multi-room renovation that requires permits and structural changes.. Related services: kitchen remodeling, bathroom remodeling, whole-home remodeling, interior home remodeling, load-bearing wall removal, floor plan redesign. Locations served: Las Vegas, North Las Vegas, Henderson, Summerlin, Paradise, Spring Valley, Enterprise, Skye Canyon, Southwest Las Vegas, MacDonald Highlands.
