Guest Bathroom Remodeling | Simply Construction

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-09-21

Upgrade your guest suite or hall bath with professional guest bathroom remodeling services from the experts at Simply Construction. A well-designed guest bathroom provides comfort for visitors while adding value to your property. Simply Construction offers complete guest bathroom remodeling for homes in Las Vegas, Henderson, and surrounding areas. We handle everything from simple updates to full renovations, including durable tile flooring, vanity installation, and lighting upgrades that make small spaces feel bright and welcoming.

Our team understands that guest bathrooms often need to balance style with ease of maintenance. With more than 30 years of construction experience, we manage all demolition, plumbing coordination, and final finishes. As a licensed, bonded, and insured contractor, we ensure every guest bath update is completed safely, on schedule, and in full compliance with local building codes. Common situations: Updating a dated hall bathroom for guests before a major event or holiday.; Refreshing fixtures, flooring, and paint to modernize a guest suite.; Replacing an old vanity and mirror to improve storage and style in a shared bathroom.; Converting a half-bath into a full guest bathroom with the addition of a shower.. Related services: bathroom remodeling, bathroom renovation, bathroom tile installation, bathroom vanity installation, guest suite addition, interior home remodeling. Locations served: Las Vegas, North Las Vegas, Henderson, Summerlin, Paradise, Spring Valley, Sunrise Manor.
