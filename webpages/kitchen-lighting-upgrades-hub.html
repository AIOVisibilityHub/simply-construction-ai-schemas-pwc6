Brighten your home with professional kitchen lighting upgrades designed for modern functionality and style. Proper lighting is essential for a functional kitchen, especially in the bright desert environment of Las Vegas. Simply Construction provides expert lighting upgrades that improve both the utility and atmosphere of your space. We coordinate all electrical work and installations, including recessed ceiling lights, under-cabinet task lighting, and decorative pendant fixtures for islands or dining areas.

With over 30 years of hands-on experience, our team ensures every upgrade is safely installed and compliant with Nevada building codes. Whether you need better visibility for meal prep or want to create a warm ambiance for entertaining, we help you plan a lighting layout that complements your kitchen's new or existing design. We handle the technical details so you can enjoy a brighter, more modern home. Common situations: Adding under-cabinet LED lighting to improve visibility on countertops.; Replacing old fluorescent fixtures with modern, energy-efficient recessed lighting.; Installing decorative pendant lights to highlight a kitchen island or breakfast bar.. Related services: kitchen remodeling, home modernization, custom kitchen remodeling, kitchen island installation, interior home remodeling, kitchen renovation. Locations served: Las Vegas, North Las Vegas, Summerlin, Henderson, Paradise, Spring Valley, Anthem.
