Tailored construction and design-build services for homeowners seeking high-quality custom renovations in Las Vegas. When a standard renovation isn't enough, Simply Construction offers custom home remodeling services designed to fit your unique lifestyle. We specialize in high-end transformations, including custom kitchen remodeling, luxury bathroom renovations, and whole-home modernizations. Our expertise allows us to tackle complex projects like second-story additions, primary suite expansions, and vaulted ceiling conversions that completely change the feel of your home.

We pride ourselves on managing every detail of the custom build process. As a licensed, bonded, and insured contractor, we handle the design coordination, trade scheduling, and necessary Nevada permits. Whether you are looking to build a detached guest house, a custom outdoor kitchen, or a multigenerational living suite, our team provides the craftsmanship and oversight required for a successful, high-quality custom project. Common situations: Executing a unique, high-end design for a custom kitchen or primary bathroom.; Adding a custom room addition, such as a home office, gym, or in-law suite.; Expanding the footprint of a home through structural expansions and custom additions.; Undertaking a whole-home renovation with bespoke finishes and architectural changes.. Related services: custom kitchen remodeling, luxury bathroom remodeling, home addition construction, casita construction, custom home remodeling, structural remodeling. Locations served: Las Vegas, Summerlin North, Summerlin South, Henderson, MacDonald Highlands, Southern Highlands, Seven Hills, Anthem, Paradise, The Lakes.
