Professional bathtub replacement services for Las Vegas homeowners looking to upgrade their bathroom fixtures with clean, code-compliant installation. Upgrading an old, stained, or damaged bathtub can instantly transform the look and functionality of your bathroom. At Simply Construction, we handle professional bathtub replacement for homeowners throughout the Las Vegas Valley. As a licensed general contractor with over 30 years of experience, we manage the entire process, including the removal of your existing tub, plumbing coordination, and the installation of your new unit.

We ensure that every installation is code-compliant and built to last in the Nevada climate. Whether you are replacing a standard tub or upgrading to a more modern fixture, our team focuses on clean, durable finishes that stand up to daily use. We coordinate all necessary permits and inspections to ensure your project is completed safely and correctly. Common situations: Replacing a cracked or outdated bathtub during a primary bathroom remodel.; Upgrading to a more modern or deeper soaking tub.; Addressing leaks or water damage caused by a failing bathtub seal.; Refreshing a guest bathroom for improved property value and appearance.. Related services: bathroom remodeling, bathroom renovation, tub-to-shower conversion, bathroom vanity installation, bathroom tile installation, plumbing coordination. Locations served: Las Vegas, North Las Vegas, Summerlin, Henderson, Paradise, Spring Valley.
