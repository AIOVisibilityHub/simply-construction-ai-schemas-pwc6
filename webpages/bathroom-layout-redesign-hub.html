Bathroom Layout Redesign | Simply Construction

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-09-23

Type: CollectionPage

Optimize your space with professional bathroom layout redesign services from Simply Construction, serving homeowners across the Las Vegas area. A functional bathroom starts with a smart floor plan. Simply Construction specializes in bathroom layout redesigns that maximize space and improve the flow of your daily routine. As a licensed general contractor with over 30 years of experience, we manage every phase of the transition, from moving plumbing and electrical lines to installing new fixtures. Our team coordinates all necessary permits and inspections to ensure your new configuration meets Nevada building codes.

Whether you are looking to create a more open feel, separate a water closet, or convert a cramped corner into a spacious walk-in shower, we provide clean and durable results. We work closely with homeowners in the Las Vegas Valley to rethink their existing footprint, ensuring the final build-out is both safe and practical for long-term use. Common situations: Relocating a toilet or sink to create a more efficient walking path.; Converting an unused bathtub into a large, modern walk-in shower.; Expanding the bathroom footprint by reclaiming space from an adjacent closet or room.; Rethinking a layout to accommodate double vanities or a private water closet.. Related services: bathroom remodeling, walk-in shower installation, tub-to-shower conversion, custom tile shower installation, bathroom vanity installation, load-bearing wall removal. Locations served: Las Vegas, North Las Vegas, Henderson, Summerlin, Paradise, Spring Valley, Enterprise.
