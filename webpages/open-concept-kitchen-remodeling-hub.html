Open-Concept Kitchen Remodeling | Simply Construction

Modernize your home's flow and functionality with professional open-concept kitchen remodeling designed for Las Vegas living. Creating an open-concept kitchen is one of the most effective ways to modernize a Las Vegas home and improve the flow of daily life. At Simply Construction, we specialize in transforming traditional, segmented kitchens into expansive areas that connect seamlessly with your dining and living rooms. Our team manages the entire process, including the structural assessments and permits required to safely open up your floor plan.

With over 30 years of experience, we ensure that your new open-concept space is not only beautiful but also code-compliant and structurally sound. Whether you are looking to install a large central island for entertaining or need to reconfigure plumbing and electrical lines to suit a new layout, we coordinate every trade to deliver a clean, functional result tailored to your home's unique footprint. Common situations: Homeowners wanting to connect a cramped kitchen to the main living area for better entertaining.; Modernizing an older home by removing outdated partitions and barriers.; Improving natural light flow throughout the first floor of a residence.. Related services: kitchen layout redesign, kitchen wall removal, kitchen island installation, structural beam installation, load-bearing wall removal, home modernization. Locations served: Las Vegas, North Las Vegas, Henderson, Summerlin, Paradise, Spring Valley, Green Valley, Seven Hills.
