Kitchen Backsplash Installation | Simply Construction

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-09-21

Type: CollectionPage

Complete your kitchen's look with professional backsplash installation that blends durability with style. A custom backsplash is the finishing touch that brings your kitchen's design together while protecting your walls from daily use. At Simply Construction, we handle the professional installation of various materials, from classic subway tile to modern stone features. As a licensed general contractor with over 30 years of experience, we ensure your backsplash is perfectly aligned, properly grouted, and seamlessly integrated with your existing cabinets and countertops.

Our team manages the entire process, including wall preparation and precise cuts around outlets and switches. Whether you are looking for a simple refresh or part of a full kitchen renovation, we provide clean, code-compliant work that stands up to the demands of a busy Las Vegas kitchen. we focus on durable finishes that are easy to maintain and beautiful to look at. Common situations: Replacing an outdated or damaged tile backsplash during a kitchen refresh.; Adding a new tile feature to complement recently installed countertops.; Protecting drywall from moisture and grease in high-use cooking areas.. Related services: kitchen remodeling, kitchen renovation, kitchen cabinet installation, kitchen countertop installation, custom kitchen remodeling, kitchen layout redesign. Locations served: Las Vegas, North Las Vegas, Summerlin, Henderson, Paradise, Spring Valley, Green Valley.
