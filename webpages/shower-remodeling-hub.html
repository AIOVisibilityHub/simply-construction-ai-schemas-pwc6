Shower Remodeling | Simply Construction

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-09-22

Type: CollectionPage

Expert shower remodeling services to upgrade your bathroom with custom tile, modern fixtures, and professional waterproofing. Simply Construction specializes in shower remodeling that transforms old, cramped stalls into spacious, modern features. We handle everything from custom tile work and waterproof membrane installation to plumbing upgrades and fixture placement. With more than three decades of hands-on construction experience in Las Vegas, we ensure your new shower is built to last and compliant with all local building requirements.

Our team focuses on clean execution and high-quality materials. We can help you choose the right tile patterns, benches, and niche placements to create a personalized experience. From simple refreshes to complete structural changes, we provide the expertise needed to manage demolition and installation safely and efficiently. Common situations: Replacing cracked or leaking tile in an existing shower stall.; Enlarging a small shower to create a more luxurious experience.; Upgrading outdated shower fixtures and valves for better performance.. Related services: bathroom remodeling, bathroom renovation, walk-in shower installation, custom tile shower installation, tub-to-shower conversion, bathroom tile installation. Locations served: Las Vegas, North Las Vegas, Henderson, Summerlin, Paradise, Spring Valley.
